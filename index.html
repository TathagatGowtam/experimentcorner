Lab Quest · pour, mix, discover

How to play

Click a bottle on the shelf, or press 1 to 9, to pour it into the beaker. Reactions change color and award points.

Challenge: finish each mission before the timer runs out. You have three lives, and toxic reactions cost one.

Free Lab: no timer and no lives. Experiment freely, use the universal indicator to read pH, and fill your notebook.

Discovered compounds join the shelf, so you can use them in new reactions.
